How do I export a chat from WhatsApp?
  1. Open the chat in WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat.
    iPhone: tap the contact/group name → Export Chat.
  3. Choose Without media.
  4. Send the file to your computer. If you get a .zip file, unzip it and pick the .txt inside.

🔒 Your chat is read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere.
